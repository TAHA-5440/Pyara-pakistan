## 1. Document purpose

This PRD defines what **Pyara Pakistan** is, who it serves, what it sells, and what the website must do.

The attached infographic is a **service reference only**. It describes the work Pyara Pakistan delivers to clients. This document turns that offering into a product: a public website that explains the services, builds trust, captures leads, and converts offline businesses into paying clients.

This PRD is the source of truth for design, content, and engineering of the first website.

---

## 2. Product summary

**Pyara Pakistan** helps traditional offline businesses in Pakistan move online and get customers — not just views.

The company packages branding, social media, content, websites, ads, CRM, reputation, and analytics into a clear 4-level service system. The website is the storefront for that system.

**One-line product:**  
A Pakistani digital-growth website where local businesses choose a service level, request a consultation, and get a complete offline-to-online transformation.

**Positioning statement:**  
For owners of shops, clinics, agencies, and local companies who still rely on walk-ins and word of mouth, Pyara Pakistan is the digital partner that sets up their online identity, attracts quality leads, and turns those leads into customers.

**Tagline (primary):**  
We don’t just get you views. We get you customers.

**Supporting line:**  
Offline business to online — complete digital transformation.

---

## 3. Problem statement

Most Pakistani businesses still operate primarily offline:

- They have a shop, clinic, office, or workshop, but no professional digital identity.
- Social profiles are incomplete, unused, or personally owned by staff.
- They have no website, catalogue, or WhatsApp inquiry system.
- They spend on ads (or want to) without a landing page, follow-up process, or CRM.
- Owners do not know which services they actually need, so they buy random posts or random ads.
- Leads come in on WhatsApp and get lost because nobody tracks them.

The result is low visibility, weak trust, wasted ad spend, and slow growth.

Pyara Pakistan exists to replace that confusion with a structured path:

**Identity → Presence → Content → Leads → Follow-up → Sales → Growth**

## 4. Goals

### 4.1 Business goals

| Brand awareness | Become a recognizable name for “offline to online” in Pakistan | 10,000+ site visits |

2. Let a busy business owner understand **what they get** at each of the 4 levels.
3. Capture leads through WhatsApp, forms, and callback requests.

The first website is **not**:

- A client self-serve portal with login dashboards

- A marketplace connecting random freelancers
- An e-commerce store for digital products
- A blog-only content site with no conversion path

Those may come later. v1 is a high-converting service website.

## 5. Brand

### 5.1 Name

**Pyara Pakistan**

Use this spelling everywhere on the product: logo, metadata, domain copy, invoices, WhatsApp greeting.

### 5.2 Brand meaning

The name should feel warm, local, and proud — “pyara” as affection for Pakistani businesses — while the product itself feels professional and results-driven.

Tone mix:

- Warm and Pakistani in greeting and examples
- Direct and commercial in offers and CTAs
- Simple Urdu-English bilingual where it helps owners, not as decoration

### 5.3 Voice

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Speak like a growth partner | Speak like a generic agency |
| Use plain language: leads, customers, WhatsApp, Google | Use jargon: omnichannel, synergy, digital disruption |
| Promise systems and follow-up | Promise “going viral” |
| Show local industries | Copy foreign SaaS landing pages |
| Be specific: “we set up Google Business, ads, and CRM” | Be vague: “we do marketing” |

### 5.4 Visual direction (from reference, not a copy)

The infographic used navy, gold, and white. The website should feel:

- Trustworthy (navy / deep green / charcoal)
- Premium but accessible (gold or warm accent, not luxury-only)
- Icon-led and scannable
- Built for mobile first — most owners will open it on a phone

Visual motif: **shop / clinic / office → phone, WhatsApp, website, customers**.

## 6. Users

### 6.1 Primary user: Offline business owner

**Who:** Owner or decision-maker of a local business that currently depends on physical location, referrals, or sales staff.

**Context:** Pakistan (starting with major cities: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, then Faisalabad, Peshawar, Multan, and others).

- Get inquiries every week

- Agencies that disappear after taking payment
- Looking unprofessional compared with competitors
- Complicated retainers they cannot understand

Phone rings, WhatsApp fills with real inquiries, Google shows their business, and sales increase.

A manager, receptionist, or owner’s child who researches vendors and books the first call. They need clear packages, prices or starting ranges, and a simple “Book a call / WhatsApp us” path.

- Large enterprises needing RFPs and 12-month brand campaigns
- People wanting a free DIY course
- International brands with no Pakistan presence
- Job seekers (careers can be a later page)

Priority verticals for copy, examples, and case studies:

1. Real estate
2. Restaurants and cafes
3. Salons and beauty
4. Car dealers
5. Construction companies
6. Clinics and healthcare
7. Education businesses
8. Boutiques and fashion
9. Manufacturers
10. Gyms and fitness
11. Photography studios

The website should make any local business feel included, but these 11 should have dedicated examples, icons, and later landing pages.

## 8. Service catalog (what Pyara Pakistan delivers)

These 10 areas are the company’s delivery catalogue. The website must present them as modules that map into the 4 paid levels.

### 8.1 Business setup

**Purpose:** Give the business a professional identity before any ads run.

Includes:

- Logo and basic brand identity (colors, fonts, usage)
- WhatsApp Business setup (profile, catalog basics, labels, greeting)
- Professional business email
- Digital visiting card (shareable link / QR)

### 8.2 Social media setup

**Purpose:** Own the platforms where Pakistani customers already search and scroll.

Platforms:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube
- Google Business Profile
- LinkedIn

Includes: account creation or recovery, branding, bio, highlights/cover, NAP consistency (name, address, phone), and basic posting setup.

### 8.3 Content creation

**Purpose:** Replace random phone photos with content that sells.

Includes:

- Professional photos
- Reels / Shorts / videos
- Educational content (how-to, FAQs, offer explainers)
- Customer testimonials
- Behind the scenes
- Owner / founder personal branding content

Client outcome: the page looks active, trustworthy, and local.

### 8.4 Online presence

**Purpose:** Create a digital shopfront that can capture inquiries 24/7.

Includes:

- Website or landing page
- Digital catalogue / services list
- WhatsApp click-to-chat
- Contact forms
- Google Maps listing
- Online inquiry system

Client outcome: a stranger can understand the offer and contact the business in under 30 seconds.

### 8.5 Digital marketing

**Purpose:** Buy attention from the right people in the right area.

Channels:

- Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
- Google Ads
- TikTok Ads

Includes: campaign setup, retargeting, local area targeting, creative testing, budget guidance.

Client outcome: consistent inquiries instead of hoping organic posts work.

Flow:

1. Define target audience

**Purpose:** Stop leads dying in WhatsApp.

Includes:

Especially important for real estate, clinics, education, and consultants.

### 8.9 Online reputation

**Purpose:** Make new customers feel safe choosing this business.

Includes:

## 9. Commercial model: 4 service levels

The website sells **levels**, not a confusing à-la-carte menu. Modules from section 8 are bundled as follows.

### Level 1 — Digital Setup

**Promise:** Create your online identity and basic presence.

**Typical inclusions:**

- Business setup (logo/brand basics, WhatsApp Business, email, digital card)
- Social media setup
- Google Business / Maps
- Basic online presence (simple landing page or Link-in-bio grade page)

**Best for:** New businesses, shops with no profiles, owners who need a foundation.

**Website CTA:** Start with Digital Setup

### Level 2 — Content & Branding

**Promise:** Look professional and start attracting attention.

**Typical inclusions:**

- Everything needed from Level 1 if missing
- Content creation (photos, reels, educational posts, testimonials)
- Personal branding for the owner
- Online reputation kickoff (review request system)

**Best for:** Businesses that exist online but look amateur or inactive.

**Website CTA:** Upgrade to Content & Branding

### Level 3 — Marketing & Lead Generation

**Promise:** Run ads that bring qualified leads.

**Typical inclusions:**

- Landing page / WhatsApp funnel
- Paid ads (Meta / Google / TikTok / YouTube as relevant)
- Audience + local targeting
- Retargeting
- Lead capture system

**Best for:** Businesses ready to spend on ads and needing inquiries now.

**Website CTA:** Get Leads

### Level 4 — Complete Digital Transformation

**Promise:** The full growth system.

**Typical inclusions:**

- Branding + content + social + website
- Ads + lead generation
- CRM and follow-up
- Reputation management
- Analytics and monthly growth reviews

**Positioning:** Flagship offer. “Complete Growth System.”

**Website CTA:** Book a transformation consult

### Packaging rules for the website

1. Show all 4 levels on one comparison section.
2. Recommend a starting level with a short quiz or “Not sure? WhatsApp us.”
3. Allow custom / industry packages later, but v1 copy should stay at 4 levels.
4. Price may be “starting from” or “book a quote” if final PKR prices are not locked. The PRD assumes quotes for Level 3–4 and published starting prices for Level 1–2 if available.
5. Always show a path to upgrade: L1 → L2 → L3 → L4.

## 12. Functional requirements

### 12.1 Lead capture

The website must collect leads in at least three ways:

1. **WhatsApp click-to-chat**  
   Pre-filled message, e.g.  
   `Assalamualaikum, I want to know about Pyara Pakistan packages for my business.`

2. **Contact / quote form**

   Required fields:

   - Full name
   - Phone (Pakistan format)
   - City
   - Business type / industry
   - Current online presence (none / social only / website / ads already)
   - Interested level (1–4 or not sure)
   - Message

   Optional:

   - Business name
   - Instagram / Facebook URL
   - Monthly ad budget range

3. **Click-to-call** on mobile

Every lead submission must:

- Show a success state
- Notify the team (email and/or WhatsApp)
- Store the record (spreadsheet, email inbox, or CRM in v1)
- Include UTM / source if present (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`)

### 12.2 WhatsApp as primary conversion channel

WhatsApp is the default close path for Pakistani SMEs.

Requirements:

- Floating WhatsApp button on all pages
- Header WhatsApp on mobile
- Package and service pages include “Discuss this on WhatsApp”
- Deep links with encoded intro text that names the page (e.g. Level 4)

### 12.3 Package selection

Users can:

- Compare 4 levels
- Open a level page
- Click “Request this package”
- Land on contact form with the package pre-selected, or WhatsApp with package name filled

### 12.4 Industry personalization (v1 light)

Each industry page uses the same layout with swapped:

- Headline
- Pain points
- Recommended starting level
- Example deliverables
- CTA

Example: Real estate page emphasizes owner branding, lead follow-up, and listing ads. Restaurant page emphasizes Google Maps, photos, Reels, and local ads.

### 12.5 Content and SEO basics

Every public page needs:

- Unique title and meta description
- H1
- Open Graph image
- Semantic headings
- Fast mobile load

Target search themes (Urdu + English mix in content, English URLs):

- Digital marketing for local business Pakistan
- Google Business setup
- Facebook Instagram ads Karachi / Lahore
- Website for shop / clinic
- Offline to online business

### 12.6 Analytics (site)

Track:

- Page views
- CTA clicks (WhatsApp, Call, Form)
- Form starts vs submissions
- Package page views
- Industry page views
- Scroll depth on home

Tools for v1: Google Analytics 4 + Meta Pixel + TikTok Pixel (placeholders if IDs not ready).

### 12.7 Admin / operations (v1 minimum)

No full CMS required if content is coded, but the team must be able to:

- Change WhatsApp number, phone, and email without a full rebuild (config file or env)
- Receive form leads
- Update testimonials and case studies with low effort (markdown or simple data files)

A headless CMS is a v2 item unless content volume is high from day one.

## 13. Key user journeys

### Journey A — Shop owner with nothing online

1. Sees a Pyara Pakistan ad or Google result.
2. Lands on home, recognizes “offline to online.”
3. Scrolls service icons and 4 levels.
4. Identifies with Level 1.
5. Taps WhatsApp.
6. Team qualifies and books a visit/call.
7. Sold Level 1, with a path to Level 2 in 30 days.

**Success metric:** WhatsApp click from Home or Packages.

### Journey B — Clinic already on Instagram, no leads

1. Lands on `/industries/clinics` or `/packages/level-3`.
2. Sees lead flow diagram.
3. Submits form with “already have social, need patients.”
4. Team proposes Level 3 (ads + landing + WhatsApp) or Level 4 if follow-up is broken.

**Success metric:** Form submit with industry + level.

### Journey C — Referred owner, high intent

1. Opens site from a forwarded WhatsApp link.
2. Skips to Contact.
3. Calls or WhatsApps immediately.

**Success metric:** Time-to-first-contact under 2 minutes on site; team reply under 15 minutes.

## 15. UX and design requirements

1. Mobile-first. Most traffic will be Android phones.
2. Large tap targets for WhatsApp and Call.
3. Urdu-friendly later: avoid tiny English-only legal walls in the hero.
4. Icon system for 10 services and 11 industries (custom or consistent icon set).
5. Comparison table for 4 levels that works on mobile (cards stacked, not an unreadable wide table).
6. No stock-photo clutter of random global offices. Prefer Pakistani business imagery when available.
7. Accessibility: contrast, alt text, keyboard focus, form labels.
8. Performance: LCP under 2.5s on 4G; images compressed; no heavy carousels on hero.

## 16. Technical requirements

### 16.1 Stack (already chosen)

- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

### 16.3 Quality bar

- Responsive: 360px, 768px, 1024px, 1440px
- SEO metadata on all pages
- 404 page on-brand with CTA
- Sitemap and `robots.txt`
- Canonical URLs
